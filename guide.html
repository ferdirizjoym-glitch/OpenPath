Accessibility Guide

Discover what accessibility features mean, understand their purpose, and learn how to check a destination before visiting.

Understanding Accessibility

Accessibility means making places, facilities, and services easier to access and use for people with different abilities and needs.

Not everyone experiences places in the same way. Some visitors may use wheelchairs or mobility aids, while others may have visual, hearing, or other accessibility needs.

Open Path provides information about accessibility features in destinations around Olongapo City to help visitors make informed decisions and plan their visits.

Explore Accessibility Features

Learn about the different accessibility features you may encounter in establishments and public spaces.

Wheelchair Ramp
A sloped pathway that provides an alternative to stairs or raised entrances. It helps wheelchair users and people with mobility limitations move between different levels.
Handrails
Support rails installed along stairs, ramps, and other areas to help visitors maintain balance, stability, and support while moving.
Elevator
A lift that allows visitors to move between different floors without using stairs. Accessible elevators may include features such as reachable controls, Braille markings, and audible floor announcements.
Priority Seating
Designated seating intended for individuals who may need additional assistance or consideration, including persons with disabilities, senior citizens, and pregnant individuals.
Braille or Tactile Indicators
Raised characters, Braille markings, or textured surface indicators that help people with visual impairments identify information and navigate their surroundings.
Accessible Restroom
A restroom designed to accommodate people with disabilities. It may include wider doorways, grab bars, accessible fixtures, and sufficient space for maneuvering.
High Contrast Signage
Signs that use contrasting colors between text, symbols, and backgrounds to make information easier to distinguish and read, particularly for people with low vision.
Accessible Parking
Designated parking spaces for persons with disabilities, usually positioned to provide more convenient access to entrances and pathways.

Understanding Accessibility Status

Open Path uses status indicators to show the availability of accessibility features at each destination.

Available
The feature has been confirmed to be present at the destination.
Unavailable
The feature has been confirmed to be absent at the destination.
Not Verified
There is insufficient information to confirm whether the feature is present or absent.
Not Applicable
The feature does not reasonably apply to the particular destination or its layout.

Know Before You Go

Before visiting a destination, remember to:

  • Review the accessibility features and their corresponding statuses.
  • Check the additional accessibility notes for important details.
  • Confirm information with the establishment when necessary.
  • Check operating hours and contact information before visiting.
  • Consider your individual accessibility requirements when choosing a destination.

Accessibility information may change over time. We encourage visitors to contact the destination directly to confirm specific accessibility requirements before their visit.
