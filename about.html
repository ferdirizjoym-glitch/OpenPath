About Open Path

Open Path is an accessibility-focused destination directory designed to help individuals discover places around Olongapo City and learn about their accessibility features.

Through an interactive map and organized destination information, Open Path aims to make exploring local establishments more informed, convenient, and inclusive.

Why We Created Open Path

Finding information about the accessibility of public places and establishments can be challenging. Visitors may not always know whether a destination has wheelchair ramps, accessible restrooms, elevators, or other facilities that accommodate their needs. This can make planning a visit difficult, particularly for persons with disabilities, senior citizens, and individuals with mobility limitations.

We created Open Path to make accessibility information easier to discover and understand, helping people make more informed decisions before visiting a destination.

Our Mission and Vision

Mission

To make local exploration more inclusive by providing accessible, organized, and useful information about local destinations and their facilities.

Vision

To encourage a community where everyone can explore, participate, and experience local destinations with confidence and independently.

Who We Serve

Persons with Disabilities (PWDs)

Discover destinations with accessibility features that may support their needs, helping them plan visits with greater confidence and independence.

Caregivers and Companions

Prepare for visits by checking accessibility features in advance and anticipating the needs of the people they accompany.

Senior Citizens

Find information about facilities that can make visiting destinations safer, more comfortable, and more convenient.

Residents and Visitors

Explore local destinations around Olongapo City and access useful information to make more informed travel and visiting decisions.
